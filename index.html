What is FreeCell?

FreeCell is a solitaire game where you build all 52 cards onto four foundation piles. Every card is dealt face up, so skill and planning matter far more than luck, and almost every deal can be won. It is a great quick puzzle you can replay again and again.

How to play FreeCell

  1. Goal: complete the four foundations

    Build each suit on the four foundations at the top left, from Ace up to King. Once all 52 cards are on the foundations, you win.

  2. Move cards between columns

    On the eight columns, stack cards in descending order and alternating colors. For example, a red 7 can go on a black 8. Any card can be placed in an empty column.

  3. Park cards in the free cells

    The four free cells at the top right each hold a single card temporarily. Use them to get out of a jam, and empty them again as soon as you can.

  4. Move several cards at once

    A run of alternating-color cards can be moved together. The maximum is (empty free cells + 1) × 2 to the power of the number of empty columns.

  5. Send cards up and double-tap

    Drag a card, or tap it and then tap where it should go. Double-click (or double-tap) a card to send it to a foundation or the nearest legal spot automatically.

Tips to win more often

  • Find where the Aces and 2s are buried first and dig those columns out.
  • Empty columns are powerful: they greatly increase how many cards you can move at once.
  • Try to keep one or two free cells open at all times.
  • Stuck? Use Undo to step back and try a different order.

Frequently asked questions

Is FreeCell free to play here?

Yes, it is completely free. There is no sign-up and nothing to install - just open the page and play.

Can every FreeCell deal be won?

Almost every deal can be won with the right sequence of moves, because all cards are visible from the start. If you get stuck, use Undo and try another order.

How many cards can I move at once?

You can move (empty free cells + 1) cards, doubled for every empty column. With three empty free cells and no empty column, that is up to 4 cards.

How do I play on a phone?

Drag cards to move them, or tap a card and then tap its destination. Double-tap a card to send it to a foundation or the nearest legal spot.

Can I play in fullscreen or install it as an app?

Use the fullscreen button in the top bar. You can also install the game from the menu (or use Add to Home Screen on iPhone) to open it like an app.

Will my game be saved if I close the page?

Yes. Your progress is saved in your browser automatically, so you can continue on the same device and browser.

Can I change the sound or the background?

Open the menu and choose Options to turn sound on or off and pick a table background.
